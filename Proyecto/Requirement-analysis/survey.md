# 🧾 Personal Profile Survey

## 🎓 Education Level

1. What is your academic background?
2. Are you currently studying?
3. What are you studying and where?
4. Do you think having a degree will help you achieve success?

---

## 🧍 Lifestyle

1. Do you consider that you have good or bad habits?
   - If so, mention one good and one bad habit.
2. Do you have a daily routine?
   - If yes, what is it like?
3. Do you work?
   - If so, how many hours a day do you work?
4. Do you have a balance between studies, work, and leisure?
   - If so, do you consider it good, average, or poor?

---

## 🎨 Interests and Hobbies

1. Do you have any hobbies?
2. What is the activity you do the most?
3. What topics of interest do you most often see on social media?
   - (e.g., sports, artists, technology, science, etc.)
4. Mention _5 activities_ you dislike or would never do.

---

## ❤️ Values and Beliefs

1. Do you practice any religion?
   - If not, do you consider yourself an atheist?
2. What do you think is the _most important value_ a person should have?
3. Are you tolerant of other people’s beliefs?
4. Do you think all people are equal?
   - Why or why not?

---

## 🎯 Goals and Desires

1. Do you have goals for the upcoming year?
   - Mention them.
2. What is your _biggest goal or desire_ in life?
3. Did you have any goals this year that you couldn’t achieve?
   - Mention them.
4. Do you believe that goals and desires can be achieved through your own effort?

---

## 🌱 Needs

1. Do you have everything you need to effectively carry out your daily activities?
2. What do you need to feel happy?
3. Do you think that if you managed to meet all your needs, you would become the best version of yourself?
   - Why or why not?
4. Do you believe that having needs helps people make an effort to improve themselves?
   - Why or why not?

---

## ⛓️ Limitations

1. Do you consider that you have any limitations in performing your daily activities?
2. Do you think you have _areas for improvement_?
   - If yes, what are they?
3. What do you consider your _greatest limitation_, and why?
4. Do you think you can overcome your limitations?
